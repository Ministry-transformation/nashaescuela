Candy Spark presents confectionery from several international brands and categories through one Ukrainian importer and online shop. The official store describes the company as an official and exclusive importer for selected brands; keep that claim tied to the brands named on the source site and do not broaden it.

The official site lists delivery across Ukraine and customer support Monday through Saturday, 09:00–18:00, with the online shop available 24/7.
